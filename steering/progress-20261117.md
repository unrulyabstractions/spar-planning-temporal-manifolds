
**Audit of the extraction**, done before fitting (ad-hoc script over `index.jsonl` and `responses.jsonl`):
- **Replay mismatches.** All 18 are at margin −0.125, one bf16 step: `Counter({-0.125: 18})`. This is the near-tie
  tolerance in `extraction_design/README.md` §2. Mismatch uids: x0013, x0076, x0174, x0188, x0268, x0390, x0401,
  x0412, x0416, x0460, v009-near-first, v019/v022/v032/v049/v065/v086/v095-far-first.
- **Margins are small overall.** `{0.125: 165, 0.25: 148, 0.0: 81, -0.125: 18}` at or below 0.25 (412 of 700); median
  0.25. On this model, answers at the label slot are nearly tied.
- **Label sites.**
  - `label_index` is 3 or 4 (350 each); the (prelabel, label) tokens are `(':',' b') 233, (' ','2') 217, (' ','1') 133,
    (':',' a') 117`. The prelabel token depends on the label style, as in §4 of the design.
  - The label token differs from the parsed label character for 0 of 700.
  - Decoded spot checks: `x0001 'I choose:' [ ' a' ] '). My reasoning' | parsed a)`; the same shape for x0002 (`'2'`),
    x0013 (a mismatch prompt), v009-near-first (`'1'`) and v100-far-first (`' b'`).
