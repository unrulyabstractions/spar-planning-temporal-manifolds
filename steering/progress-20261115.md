## Gotchas found (what the evaluation team would hit)

1. **No `/workspace` in this image.** The team justfile assumes `/workspace/SPAR/...`. We used `/root/work`.
2. **`uv sync --frozen` installs no extras.** pytest is the optional extra `dev`, and prompt_bank's torch and
   transformers are the extra `local`. A plain sync gave a prompt_bank venv without torch, and `baseline.py` died with
   `ModuleNotFoundError: No module named 'torch'`.
   - Fix: setup now follows each README, `--all-extras` for prompt_bank and `--extra dev` for steering.
3. **Real-model tests skip silently on a fresh box.** They need the models in the Hugging Face cache (Qwen3.5-0.8B
   weights, Qwen3.5-2B and 27B tokenizers). The `weights` stage now downloads those too.
4. **Batch 16 runs out of memory** with Gemma 4 31B on 80 GB at 4096 new tokens:
   `CUDA out of memory. Tried to allocate 1.17 GiB ... 1.03 GiB is free`, inside SDPA's `repeat_kv`.
   - Arithmetic: about 21 GiB free after weights; about 1.1 GB of key-value cache per sequence at ~3k tokens; plus
     `repeat_kv` temporaries.
   - Default is now 8. The 16 was my sizing error; baseline.py's own default is 4.
5. **A failed run blocks a retry with different settings.** The OOM left a `run.json` recording batch_size 16 and no
   replies, and baseline.py correctly refused to resume at batch 8. I deleted the directory (it held only run.json).
   batch_size belongs in the settings, because greedy outputs depend on padding.
6. **Throughput is far below my earlier estimate.**
   - Measured: 4096 decode steps for a batch of 8 in about 605 s, i.e. about 148 ms per step.
   - Every batch so far had a reply that ran to the cap, so each batch costs the full cap.
   - At this rate the full 700-prompt baseline at cap 4096 is about 88 batches × 10 min ≈ **14–15 h**, and longer at
     the spec's 8192 cap. The "about 2.5 h" in the draft reply to Augusto is wrong and must be corrected.
   - 3/16 replies hit 4096 with no answer, against about 750 tokens in the 2-prompt local smoke.
7. **Host bandwidth charges dominate a short run.** This host charges $40/TB in both directions: 64.1 GB downloaded
   cost $2.504 of the first $2.798. Every Gemma box downloads about 63 GB, so filter offers on `inet_down_cost`.
   Destroy, don't stop: a stopped instance keeps billing its disk ($0.278/h for 200 GB).
8. **Bug in my own audit stage, now fixed.**
   - The first audit decoded the wrong tokens: it computed the offset as `label_index - (seq_len - len(generated))`,
     but `label_index` already indexes the generated ids, and `seq_len` stops at the label.
   - It still printed "ok" for all 13, because it compared only extract's `label_token` field, not the token it
     decoded.
   - Now it checks the decoded token against both the field and the parsed choice; negative control above.
   - The extraction itself was right.
9. **fit.py has no minimum class size.** With 13 prompts it wrote a fit whose CAA long class is 1 prompt. Not changed
   here: whether a minimum should be enforced (refuse, and log `refused`) is a decision for Alan.

## Why it took 92 minutes, not 60

- **About 10 minutes:** the OOM restart and the extras fix.
- **About 35 minutes:** a monitoring error of mine.
  - The background "wait until the chain finishes" loop tested `! pgrep -f "bash steering/tools/vast_smoke"` over
    ssh. The pattern matched the ssh command's own `bash -c` line, so the condition never became true. The chain had
    in fact finished at 12:56:54.
  - An earlier `pkill -f vast_smoke.sh` likewise killed its own ssh session (exit 255).
  - Lesson for any box script: match processes by PID or a `[b]racket` pattern, and give every wait a wall-clock
    deadline.
  - Cost of the overrun: about 35 min × $1.38/h ≈ $0.80.
