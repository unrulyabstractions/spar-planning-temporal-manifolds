## The failed check: curve-displace at the label site

Rerun on the box before destroying it, same bundle, same 4 prompts, 64 tokens, every token edited:

```
x = +4.0: changed 0/4 replies      x = -4.0: changed 0/4 replies      x = +1.0: changed 2/4 replies (passes)
```

So the hook is applied (x = 1 changes replies, and the same hook passes for the other eight bundles). What the
check assumes is that a 4-decade shift is a strong edit. For this curve it is not. From `manifold.npz`, computed
locally:

```
label    curve: length 79.3, end to end 22.6 (activation units; typical norm 304)
         |c(t+1) - c(t)| median 5.0, max 15.6;   |c(t+4) - c(t)| median 17.6, max 23.2;   from t = -3.46: 10.0 vs 8.0
prelabel curve: length 175.2, end to end 14.1;   |c(t+4) - c(t)| median 18.0, max 34.5
```

The curve is folded (3.5 times longer than its end-to-end distance at the label site, 12 times at prelabel), and no
displacement along it exceeds 8% of the activation norm. From some starting points a 4-decade shift moves the
activation less than a 1-decade shift does.

**Not verified:** which t-hat the edited tokens had in those replies. That needs the model. The explanation above
is consistent with the three runs, not proven by them.

## Things this run shows that matter downstream

1. **The 42 capped prompts are not a random sample:** 39 are in the extraction split's `none` regime (14% of it), 3 in
   `both`, none in the verification split. The fit's `none` class is the 241 that finished.
2. **At the prelabel token the choice is already fixed.** The written letter is the top prediction on all 659
   prompts, and the gap between the two letters' logits is at least 13. An edit there has to overturn that; the
   prelabel CAA gate at x = 4 changed 1 of 4 choices.
3. **Within-regime CAA directions point against the choice direction** in `both` and `none` at both sites
   (cos -0.56 to -0.76), while the plain CAA points with it (+0.889, +0.680). The plain CAA's alignment with choice
   comes from the regime contrast.
4. **The prelabel curve does not generalise:** held-out Spearman -0.020, against +0.324 at the label site.
5. **Covariance-matched random directions reach |cos| 0.76 with CAA** at the label site (isotropic: 0.038).
