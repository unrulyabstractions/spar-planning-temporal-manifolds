Horizon eval prompts

Hand-checked samples for the realism tiers and robustness banks, before scaling up. Correct answers use the rule max_value_within_H: among options that pay out within the horizon H, the largest payout wins. Nothing here has been run through a model yet.

Open decisions before scaling up
  1. The horizon must decide the answer. Some other H must make a different option correct. Flagged on cards as "horizon never changes the answer".
  2. Options that pay after H must say they can't be cashed out early. Otherwise "an index fund held 5 years" against a 2-year need has no defensible answer. Flagged as "late option not locked".
  3. Keep risk out of the correct-answer rule, but cross it against the answer in about half of items. Flagged when the correct option is also strictly the safest.
  4. City projects: equal cost per option, or state net benefit. The rule uses total benefit, so a model that weighs cost disagrees with it.
  5. No "nothing pays in time" items in scored banks. The spec already keeps those for extraction only.
  6. Reword T3 retirement items to a hard withdrawal date. "Retire at 65" isn't a liability date; the money is spent for decades after.
  7. Scale-up: a script makes the numbers and labels, subagents write only the prose, a validator re-parses the text. The midterm spec said Haiku 5.5 generates and GPT-5.6 Luna checks; this would replace that.
