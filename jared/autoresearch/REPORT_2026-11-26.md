## Findings

**Why the 2026-11-25 Qwen3-8B run found nothing** (from saved artifacts; all three now show on the diagnostics page):
1. CAA's classes barely differ in behaviour: far chosen on 47% of the long class and 31% of the short one, because 275
   of the 346 short prompts are `none` (neither option pays off), which the model answers far 39% of the time.
   CAA's split-half reliability is 0.40; `caa-within` is noise (0.09).
2. The strength rule judges size only, so the reversed effect at x = 0.8 (-1.16) "reached" the 1-logit target.
   `--calib-rule peak` stops at the first sign change (x = 0.2 there).
3. The run used bf16 replay: batched vs one-at-a-time differed by 0.5 logits; in fp32 tonight, 1e-4.

**Tonight's run** (fp32 replay of the 2026-11-25 baseline, L24 `prelabel`, x = 0.4 chosen by the peak rule, K = 19):

| direction | meaning (logits) | letter | even (disruption) | Δlog10 H* | p vs covariance nulls |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `choice` (positive control) | +7.76 [7.33, 8.17] | -1.35 | +1.04 | +2.12 | 0.05 / 0.05 |
| `caa` | +3.31 [2.88, 3.77] | +4.49 | +3.03 | +0.35 | 0.15 / 0.25 |

The null family is strong at this strength: its 95th percentile is 4.09 logits. `choice` acts as a decision bias, as
a choice direction should (low plateau of P(far) 0.78: far chosen even where it cannot pay off; slope 3.4 → 0.45).
The regime direction (`both` minus `near_only`) calibrated to the same x and moved held-out prompts by +6.47 logits
at x = 0.4 (CAA: +1.69), but its verification-grid replay was killed, so it has no test yet.

**Corrections to what I told you earlier** (all in the log):
- CAA's cosine with the letter direction (0.33) is inside its shuffled-label null (95%: 0.39). Raw L24 mean
  differences share a few huge dimensions (one averages ~89 against a mean |x| of 1.5), so a fixed cosine threshold
  is meaningless. The replay's letter effect is real; the cosine does not explain it.
- Regime vs choice at `prompt_end` is 0.08 only against the all-prompt choice direction (dominated by `none`);
  within `near_only`/`both` it is 0.78. So "regime at prompt_end is separate from the decision" does not hold.
- The checker agent said all verification prompts use phrasing 0; 68 of 200 do.

**Bank design confounds** (details in `ConfoundCoverage.md`):
- Extraction split: horizon length and regime move together; `rewritten` is 21% of `both` vs 68% of `near_only`.
- Verification: reward multiplier 2 is 36% of `both` vs 12% of `near_only`.
- Phrasing 1 ("best over that horizon"): Qwen3-8B `both` accuracy 0.26 vs 0.48 for the rest (Fisher p = 0.02).
- `both` accuracy is 0.43 overall: the model takes the smaller, sooner option on most prompts where both pay off.
- Later is always larger in every bank, so "prefer more" and "prefer later" cannot be told apart.
