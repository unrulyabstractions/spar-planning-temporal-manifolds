## Design (pre-registered: written before any GPU number of tonight's run)

No forking paths: every prompt set has one job.
- **Fit:** extraction-split prompts in `splits/reserved_horizons.jsonl` role `fit` (419; 39 of the 48 horizon values).
- **Screen (select):** role `test` of the same split, scored regimes only (near_only 19 + both 11, readable ones):
  prompts at 9 horizon values the fit never saw. This is the "different horizon spacing" check: a method must move
  the choice at horizons between the ones it was fitted on.
- **Confirm:** the T0 verification grid (178 readable items), used once, for the winner only, with K = 19
  covariance nulls at the winner's own layer, site, strength and write span. fp32.
- **Generalization (after confirm, if time):** the frozen winner (same layer, site, strength rule, span) on the
  `quick_system_prompt` transfer set (`evaluation/transfer_eval.py`). No re-tuning.

Screen grid (cheapest-first priority order; the screen stops starting new configs at its deadline):
- layers 20, 16, 24, 28, 12 (Qwen3-8B has 36; L24 alone so far);
- read sites `prompt_end` (horizon is decodable there: curve held-out-horizon ρ 0.6-0.9) and `prelabel`;
- methods: `caa` on fit set `nonone` (CAA classes without `none`), `regime` (both − near_only), `trend` (new:
  covariance of the activation with log10 H after removing each regime's mean from both: a horizon direction with the
  regime part taken out), `probe` (new: ridge decoder of log10 H, for comparison), `curve-displace` with k = 4 and
  k = 8 (prompt_end only); `choice` at L24 as the positive control (never a candidate);
- write span `all` (every token, as so far) and `prompt` (prompt tokens only: the patch reading) for prompt_end
  methods.
- Strength grid: push x ∈ {0.1, 0.2, 0.4, 0.8} × typical norm; curve Δ ∈ {0.5, 1, 2, 4} decades; stop a config's
  sweep at its first unhealthy strength or sign change.

**Screen score S** (pre-registered): the peak rule of `replay_eval.choose_strength` on the order-balanced mean odd
effect (logits, + = toward far = toward longer horizon) among healthy strengths (label-mass rule of calibrate).
Also recorded, not used for selection: label-mass ratio at the chosen strength, odd effect by regime and by option
order (letter part). **Winner:** the highest S among candidates (not `choice`/`reward`). Ties within 0.25 logits:
the smaller strength.
