  logits [0.42, 0.63] and does not move the switch point (Δlog10 H* 0.00).** That is `patch:text`: rewrite the horizon

  pre-registered "a perfect edit shifts H* by 1 decade" was wrong; corrected in analyze.py and patching.py.
- **The earlier phrase patches that worked (E14 +9.9, E16 +5.2 with the donor's number written 24/24, E20 +7.9 to
  +29.1 near log-odds in 13 of 14 eval types, all L16) regenerated the reasoning.** The patched model restates the
  donor's horizon in its reasoning and the answer follows. Replay blocks that path, so tonight's patch numbers are a

## What this means

1. Under teacher forcing, prompt-side horizon edits are capped near +0.5-0.7 logits, because the replayed reasoning
   dominates the answer. Only an edit that writes into the reasoning tokens (2026-11-27's all-token push) moves
   more, and that is choice-like. To measure horizon steering at the prompt, regenerate the reasoning: a small
   generation check of `patch:text` vs a horizon-token push (gencheck already supports both). That is the next
   experiment if this line continues.
2. The curve subspace is the one fitted object that beats its random controls here, carrying ~50% of the full patch
   (at L12 in the screen, ~87% on the last token alone). Curve methods did nothing as pushes (2026-11-27), but they
   do as patch subspaces: a horizon representation sits there that a constant push does not use well.
3. Probe directions carry almost none of the patch (≤ 0.08): what a probe reads is not what the model uses.

## Caveats

- Screens have no nulls; S below ~1.5 logits is within random-direction range at these strengths.
- K = 9 / 8 nulls: p floors at 0.10, so "above all nulls" is weak evidence.
- Not kept: the horizon activations, fits and method bundles lived in the box's checkout and were destroyed with it
  (only `results/` syncs). Refitting needs a ~1 min extraction on a GPU.
- Pre-existing, not fixed: on pure-noise activations the fit diagnostics' held-out-horizon Spearman is +0.17 (trend)
  and +0.29 (probe), not ~0, so the fits' "ridge ρ" has a nonzero noise floor (likely regime demeaning before CV).
