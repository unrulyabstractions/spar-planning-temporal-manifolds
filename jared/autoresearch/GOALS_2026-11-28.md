## Design

- Read sites: `horizon_last` (the unit token, " years") and `horizon_mean` (mean over the phrase), extracted by
  extract.py at layers 12, 16, 20, 24 (the 2026-11-27 screen's best layers; L28 dropped for budget).
- Write spans: `horizon` (the phrase's tokens) and `from_horizon` (the phrase through the end of the prompt).
- Steering candidates: caa, trend, probe (no-none fit) x 2 sites x 4 layers x 2 spans = 48 configs. `regime` dropped
  (2026-11-27: a decision-bias direction, cos 0.86 with `choice`).
- Patch baselines (patching.py), source = same prompt with the horizon x 10^(±1), same unit:
  `patch:text` (rewrite the horizon, keep the reasoning: the most a stated-horizon change does with reasoning fixed),
  `patch:full` (whole state at the patched tokens, per layer: the ceiling for edits there), `patch:<method>` (the
  subspace of trend / caa / probe / curve-displace k = 4, fitted at horizon_last). Patched tokens: the phrase's last
  token (span horizon) or it through the prompt's end (span from_horizon); numbers are one token per digit, so only the
  last token aligns. A perfect horizon edit has Δlog10 H* = 1 decade. [Wrong, found after the run: the reasoning is
  teacher-forced, so even patch:text leaves H* unchanged; see REPORT_2026-11-28.md.]
- Screen (TF32 after its own check): patch baselines first, then steering in priority order (trend, caa, probe; layers
  16, 12, 20, 24), until the screen deadline.
- Select: steering winner by the 2026-11-27 rule (screen.py select). Patch to confirm (screen.py patch-select): the
  layer and span of the best `patch:full`; there, the best subspace patch.
- Confirm (fp32, T0 grid): (1) patch: text, full, the chosen subspace, K = 9 covariance-draw null bases of its
  dimension; (2) steering winner with K = 9 covariance nulls at its layer, site and span.
- Deadline: the box job ends GPU work at rent + 2.5 h; watchdog idle limit 15 min (IDLE_KILL_MIN, set by Jared).
