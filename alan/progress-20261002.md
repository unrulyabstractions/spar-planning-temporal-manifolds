## Matched-duration per-unit horizon sets (cc79a8e)

Continues the 2026-10-01 dense-horizon thread (progress-20261001.md, last entry: pooled 4-unit set). Alan asked for per-unit
sets at matched size, to separate sampling density from unit (the pooled set's per-unit curves had ≈ 467 points each).

Design (mine): matching n alone leaves the log-horizon range and density profile unmatched (days span 4.56 decades, years 2),
so each tier renders the SAME base durations in every unit, rounding to integers (pair_id = base value):
- Tier A, n = 1200 per unit: base = every month count 1..1200 (1 month–100 y), rendered as m months, round(30.4375 m) days,
  round(4.348 m) weeks. Max relative duration error vs base: days 0.0144, weeks 0.0801 (4 weeks for 1 month), months 0.
  Years excluded (integer years cannot render sub-year durations). Weeks reach 5218 (one day over 100 y).
- Tier B, n = 100 per unit: base = years 1..100, rendered as y years, 12y months, round(365.25 y) days, round(52.18 y) weeks.
  Max errors: days 0.0007, weeks 0.0034, months and years 0.
Note: base grids are uniform in the base unit, so log-horizon density rises toward the top; tiers are comparable within
themselves, not with the log-spaced 2026-10-01 sets. Same fixed scenario and default layout as the days-only set.
```
.venv/bin/python scripts/gen_dense_horizons.py data/prompts/matched_months_dwm_n1200.parquet --matched-base months --n 1200 --max 1200 --units days,weeks,months
```

Reading:
- At matched n, range and durations, days, weeks and months have nearly the same dimension (tier A: spread 0.31–2.34
  across units per cell, largest at L22 T3), and pooling them adds nothing (the pooled value lies inside the per-unit
  range at every cell). The

  dimension, density does.
- Units remain separate manifolds under exact duration matching: 95–100% of the 20-NN share the unit; an equal duration in
  another unit is 1.75–2.53 × the 20th-NN distance away and almost never a neighbour (0–6%).
- Across units, one shared curve explains little (tier A 0.15–0.41, tier B 0.06–0.20); per-unit offsets recover most of what

  and weeks in tier A too) also renders with fewer digits than days. Hypothesis, not tested: fewer digit tokens → less
  number-string variation → more curve-like. Digit count and unit are confounded by construction here.

## Digit-count control: years 1..1200 rendered plain / zero-padded / with decimals (b105243)

Alan asked for the digit-count control proposed after the matched-unit sets (years was lowest-dimensional and most
curve-like, and renders with the fewest digits). Design (mine), one capture of 3600 prompts:
- years 1..1200 (all integers; range 1–1200 y, beyond the earlier 100 y), rendered three ways: plain ("37 years", 1–4 digit
  tokens), pad4 ("0037 years", always 4), dec2 ("37.00 years"; constant ".00" appended). Unit and values fixed; pad4 and dec2
  add only constant tokens, no new varying digits. rendering column: NaN (plain) / pad4 / dec2.
- plain years 1..1200 uses exactly the digit strings of tier-A months 1..1200 (asserted), so "same digits, different unit".
  Confound: the durations differ (years 1–1200 y vs months 1 month–100 y; ranges overlap only over 1–100 y).
- pad4 equals plain for 1000..1200 (201 identical prompts — activations identical at L22 R0, 201/201).
New: fixed_scenario(horizon_render=...), gen --render; test asserts renderings keep value and unit; canonical-style dense days
texts regenerate byte-identically. `pytest -q tests` → 105 passed, 6 skipped.

## Same-range years vs months comparison = tier B (no new run)

Alan asked for years vs months restricted to the same duration range (1–100 y) at matched n, which I had proposed as needing
no new capture. Correction to that proposal: with integer years, the only years in range are 1..100 (n capped at 100), and
the only months matching those durations are 12y — exactly the tier-B design above, with the same prompt texts. Verified
(scratch check): the 100 years-1..100 prompts of runs/qwen3-14b_years_render_n1200 and the 100 months-12y prompts of
runs/qwen3-14b_matched_months_dwm_n1200 have bit-identical activations to tier B's years and months rows at L22 R0 and
L37 T3 (100/100 each, max |diff| 0). So the answer is the tier-B table: same range, durations and n, years m 6.24–12.15
vs months 11.16–16.66, interpolation-only spline R² years 0.573–0.822 vs months 0.342–0.537 — years lower-dimensional and
more curve-like, at the low power of n = 100 (estimator saturates; ordinal only).
A first version of the identity check reported impossible counts (11530 identical rows of 100): index.parquet already has a
`row` column (shard row), and renaming the reset index to `row` duplicated it, so the indices were 2-column. Fixed by using
a non-colliding name; earlier cross-capture checks used other names (prow/drow, a/b) and are unaffected.
Combined with the digit-count control (same digit strings, different ranges → years still lower-dimensional by 4.8–7.6 and
more curve-like at 3 of 4 cells): neither digit strings (tier B differs in strings, digit control matches them) nor duration
range (tier B matches it) alone explains the years effect; unit remains the candidate. Power is the weak point: the
same-range comparison has n = 100.
