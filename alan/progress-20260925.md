## Protocol

Train: condition=main, split=train configurations (20 × 2 domains), 8 training horizons, renderings per
condition. Select (layer ∈ {14,18,22,26,29,33,37}) × (position ∈ {T0,T1,T3,T5,T8,R0}) × alpha on the 4 dev
configurations (all renderings) by RMSE in log10 years; freeze; evaluate once per suite. Metrics in
log10 years: RMSE, bias, sd, median |err|, Spearman, within factor 2 (|err| ≤ 0.301), worst horizon bin.
Baselines: regex-horizon (duration in the sentence containing "horizon"; falls back to first duration),
regex-first, TF-IDF ridge on text. Bug fixed before the reported runs: the regex sentence splitter broke
on ":" and missed the structured template (0.15 → 1.00).

## Reading

1. Scenario transfer is essentially solved for explicit horizons (within-2x 0.98–0.99 on new scenarios).
2. Presentation transfer from structured-only training fails at the factor-2 level (0.34–0.39) despite
   rank order (rho 0.89–0.95); the error is mostly scatter (sd 0.55–0.66), not a constant shift.
   Including prose in training makes a single linear decoder transfer to new scenarios in new
   renderings at 0.91–0.99 within-2x: adaptation, not direct transfer.
3. Interpolation to withheld horizons works with mixed training (0.93 on training scenarios, 0.79 on
   new scenarios); 2 weeks is the hardest bin.
4. Domain transfer investment → climate keeps rank order (0.965) but only 0.57 within-2x.
5. The decoder is pulled by durations that the model's decision ignores: residual correlation with
   the distractor +0.58 (mention) / +0.74 (role) at the selected cell, and no cell is free of it,
   while chose_short shows no dependence on the distractor (coef −0.03 / −0.11; Δp_short uncorrelated).
   The scaling control (slope 0.6–0.8) says the readout is mostly absolute duration.
   → At boundary tokens, the linear horizon readout is closer to "durations in context" than to the
   operative horizon. This is the central obstacle for goal 2 and the concrete target for step 4/5.
6. Regex on explicit prompts is perfect; the activation decoder's value must come from implicit
   prompts or from tracking the operative horizon, which this result says it does not yet do.
