# Weekly update draft — Alan — for the 2026-09-23 meeting

## Update

- Built a capture + analysis pipeline (own package, local 2×4080; Qwen3-14B fits with an explicit
  layer split, 16 min per 2,000 prompts). Capture is bit-exact against the library's hidden states;
  found and documented bf16 batching noise (up to 8% relative at deep layers) and that HF's last
  hidden state is post-norm.
- Replicated the preprint's single-turn intertemporal-choice geometry on Qwen3-14B (investment
  domain, 17 horizons + null, n = 2,000, 100% format adherence):
  - PC1 ordinality |ρ| up to 0.96 at the `assistant` token (L35–37) and 0.95 at the first response
    tokens (L20–23); reward-ratio control |ρ| ≤ 0.02.
  - Behavior: short option chosen ≥ 98% for horizons ≤ 1 year, crossover ≈ 10 years, 10% at
    50 years; no-horizon prompts behave like ~30-year prompts (6.6% short).
- Three findings beyond the replication:
  1. **Decodable everywhere, PCA-visible only where nothing louder lives.** Ridge R² for log
     horizon is 0.97–0.998 at every cell tested, including positions where PC1 shows nothing
     (the newline after `assistant` has one direction carrying 99.8% of variance; the label token is
     dominated by the choice).
  2. **The horizon coordinate carries decision information beyond the prompt's inputs.** A
     second-order model of the prompt parameters predicts the choice at held-out log-loss 0.231;
     adding three PC coordinates from the prompt-side `assistant` token improves it to 0.149, with
     gains in every mixed bin (2–100 years). PCA is fit inside each CV fold (re-checked 09-23;
     identical to ±0.001). At the first response token (`I`, before the label) the choice is
     decodable at AUC ≈ 1 within bins; where the model commits is not localized.
  3. **The bin-centroid path is a planar parabola, not a line.** Top-2 centroid PCs carry 87–88% of
     centroid variance; split-half correlation 0.99. In that plane PC1 is linear in log horizon
     (R² 0.97) and PC2 is quadratic (R² 0.87–0.89 prompt-side, 0.75–0.79 response-side; linear
     R² ≈ 0) with vertex at 0.6–1.1 years. Spacing per decade of horizon is smallest at 2–5 years and
     1.6–1.8× larger at both extremes. The same two non-monotonicities appear in geometry and
     behavior (1 day placed "longer" than 3 days; 100 years folds back toward 20–30 years).
  4. **Sink directions hide the horizon from PCA.** At the newline after `assistant` one direction
     has 110–380× the std of the median direction and PC1 shows nothing; removing it exposes
     |ρ| 0.84–0.92 in the next component (L29, L37).
- Controls captured for falsifiability (results: PENDING at draft time):
  - Unit rewrite (independent sampling, preliminary): ridge trained on canonical renderings
    predicts rewritten horizons' log magnitude with R² 0.79–0.92 and does not track the written
    number (R² < 0), so the global coordinate is magnitude. But the unit displaces centroids by
    1.5–2.4 grid steps and biases the readout toward the middle (hours read ~0.7 decades longer,
    decades/centuries 0.3–1.2 decades shorter). Strong form of H1 is dead; the coordinate is
    magnitude + a unit-dependent shift (cf. Shantanu's per-unit manifolds). Behaviorally, "decades"
    renderings get ~15 points more long-option choices than "years" at 20–100 y, opposite in sign to
    the ridge residual. **Paired result (matched option parameters, 1,311 rewrites):** ridge transfer R² 0.78–0.89;
    a unit rewrite moves a sample along the path by < 1 grid step and its orthogonal displacement is
    below the same-bin single-sample floor. Yet behavior shifts with unit ("decades" −0.11 p_short,
    "centuries" −0.07: treated as *longer* than "N years"), and the along-path shift does not predict
    it; the linear readout misreads decades/centuries as *shorter*. → The path coordinate is magnitude
    (H1 refined survives), but the along-path coordinate does not account for the unit effect on
    behavior. (Whether that effect is carried by an off-path direction, and whether the coordinate is
    necessary for behavior, need interventions; the paired "floor" is a background-variation baseline
    that includes option-parameter differences, not measurement noise.)
  - Phrasing (four constraint-line phrasings): ridge trained on phrasing 0 transfers to the
    others at R² 0.79–0.90 (L14–29), 0.61 at L37 `assistant`. Within each phrasing PC1 ordinality is
    0.86–0.97, but pooled PCA at L14 R0 drops to 0.16 because phrasings form separate clusters
    (silhouette 0.77) that merge by L29 R0 (silhouette ≈ 0): "structure preserved, absolute geometry
    not", with the offsets ≈ orthogonal to the horizon direction. Behavior shifts with wording:
    overall short-choice rate 61–78% across phrasings (40–49% in the 2–100 y region).
  - Qwen3-8B on the canonical set: same ordinal, planar, end-stretched centroid path (peak |ρ| 0.93 at
    the newline after `<|im_end|>`, L26–35; planarity 0.79–0.82; split-half ≥ 0.98), but weaker
    geometry–behavior alignment (choice silhouette 0.07 vs 0.20–0.28 at 14B) and noisier behavior
    (crossover ≈ 5 y; no-horizon prompts 27% short vs 6.6% at 14B).

## What's next

- Decide direction: "characterizing activation-space structure" × "mapping behavior to geometry".
- Falsifiable hypotheses for week 4 (each with a kill criterion):
  H1 (refined) The along-path coordinate is magnitude, and unit-induced behavior changes are carried
     off-path. Test: find the direction that separates "decades" from "years" at fixed magnitude and
     check whether it predicts Δp_short across pairs; dead if that direction is within the path plane
     or if it does not predict the behavioral shift.
  H2 Planar curved path with extremeness as second axis: centroid PC2 ≈ f(|log h − log 1y|).
     Dead if the quadratic fit residual exceeds the split-half noise, or if the plane is not stable
     across layers 20–37.
  H3 Decision reads the coordinate: PC coordinates improve choice prediction beyond a rich model
     of the prompt inputs. Dead if a degree-3 input model closes the gap, or if the gain vanishes
     under the unit/phrasing controls.
  H4 Extremes are over-resolved: spacing per decade is minimal where behavior is mixed. Dead if
     an extended grid (hours … millennia) shows uniform spacing in log horizon.
- Extended horizon grid (done, 25 bins 1 min–10000 y): behavior collapses toward chance below the
  shortest option delay (1 week) and plateaus at ~27% short from 100 to 1000 y before dropping;
  geometry: the standard-range parabola replicates on the fresh sample (vertex 0.6–0.7 y), but the
  extremes leave that plane along a third direction (PC3 of the centroids 0.03 → 0.11), and the top
  end saturates beyond ~1000 y. H2/H4 refined: "planar parabola" holds within 1 day–100 y; beyond,
  the curve is at least 3-D and saturating.
