## Design as built

- 30 option configurations (unique delay/reward tuples; order alternates), each in both domains → 60
  scenarios; × 12 horizons × 3 renderings = 2,160 prompts. Splits by configuration: 20 train / 4 dev /
  6 test (both domains of a configuration share its split). Horizons: train {1 d, 1 w, 1 mo, 6 mo,
  2 y, 10 y, 30 y, 100 y}, withheld {2 w, 3 mo, 5 y, 20 y} (720 rows flagged `horizon_heldout`).
- Renderings: structured (existing template); plain prose (fixed order); varied prose = 4 sentence
  orders × 2 option/horizon phrasings, none equal to plain, variant chosen by hash of
  (config, domain, horizon) → counts 68–113 per variant. All keep the response-format sentence so
  the choice readout survives. Meaning of the horizon sentence is identical across renderings
  ("greatest benefit within/over the horizon").
- Controls (10 training configurations × 2 domains, plain prose): mention (irrelevant "established
  {D} ago", D ≠ H from the 12-grid), role ("You have {D} to prepare", D ∈ {1 h, 2 h, 3 d, 1 w}),
  factual (no task, no format), scaling (6 base horizons × factors 0.1/1/10 applied to horizon and
  both delays; rendered in grid units only, largest unit within 5% rounding; scale 1 reproduces the
  grid text exactly). 1,080 control prompts.
- Known: the 120 scale-1.0 prompts duplicate their plain main-matrix counterparts byte-for-byte
  (distinct sample_uids) — a determinism check on the activations, not a bug.

## Bugs found by tests during construction

- varied variant 0 rendered identically to plain (same order and phrasing) → orders redefined so
  none matches plain; test asserts prompt_id uniqueness.
- `scale_horizon` first minimized rounding error (→ "438 hours"), then preferred the largest unit
  (→ "1 decade" at scale 1.0, a known behavior-shifting unit) → final rule: largest unit among
  hours…years within 5% error, exact grid text at scale 1 (asserted).
- Order test searched the whole text and hit the horizon "3 months" before the option "3 months" → test
  now searches the options span.
