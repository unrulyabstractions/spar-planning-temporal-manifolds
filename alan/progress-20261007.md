## Design, behaviour-only capture, generator (ebbd2fa), analysis (ddfefa8, this commit)

- `ptm/horizons.py`: unit `quarters` (0.25 y).
- `capture.py --no-acts` (`CaptureConfig.store_acts=False`): same batched greedy generation and unpadded per-sample
  forward, hooks never armed, no shards; the choice logits use the last layer's raw residual from the forward pass (the
  same tensor the stored acts held). Index rows get shard = row = −1 and the index is rewritten every 64 rows.
  Verification, Qwen3.5-0.8B, 32 prompts (offset 900 of matched_months_years_dec2_dm_n1200), full vs --no-acts:

  weeks, 24/60/120 months, 8/20/40 quarters); horizon H/D log-uniform in [1/1.5, 1.5] (24 points, none at H = D),
  written in the horizon unit (weeks and months integer, quarters and years two decimals, as the 10-03 years cells).
  Crossed: 4 horizon units × 4 delay units × 3 D × 2 ratios (5, 20) × 2 orders × 4 phrasings.

  phrasing). Tests: renderings, text/parameter consistency, pairs differ only in the two option lines and the

Full tables in `figures/qwen3-14b_unit_boundary_n24/unit_boundary.md`, curves in `unit_boundary_curves.png`.

Results:
- **Continuity:** the 10-03 conditions (months/years, D = 10 y, ratio 5, short first, phrasing 0; 24 prompts each)
  reproduce: switch at 10.00 y for both matched cells, 8.37 y (months horizon, year delays) and 8.09 y (years horizon,
  month delays).
- **Option order dominates.** P(choose "a)") = 0.977 when the long option is listed first, 0.244 when short is first.
  With long first, P(short) below the boundary is 0.00–0.16 in every cell except years/years (0.48, 0.40 at H/D
  0.66–0.85) and months/years (0.40, 0.16).
- **Short first, the constraint is applied cleanly only for months and years.** P(short) at H/D 0.66–0.75 / just above
  1 (1.0–1.05): years/years 1.00 / 0.00, months/months 0.93 / 0.22, years/months 0.92 / 0.38, months/years 0.70 / 0.03.
  Delays in weeks or quarters: P(short) ≤ 0.26 at every H/D for every horizon unit except quarters/quarters (0.54–0.71
  below, 0.00 just above) and weeks/weeks (0.32–0.53 below).
- **Switch points censored:** pooled over order, every cell except months/months (0.747), years/years (0.837),
  months/years (0.710) and years/months (0.772) switches below the sampled range (< 0.665).
- **Phrasing:** P(short) at H/D < 0.85, short first / long first: phrasing 0 0.59 / 0.16, 1 0.46 / 0.10, 2 0.42 / 0.03,
  3 0.22 / 0.00.
- **Reasoning audit** (sampled `gen_text`): conversions are stated correctly but the comparison is wrong, e.g. "104 weeks
  is approximately 2 years, which fits within the 17-month planning horizon"; "261 weeks is approximately 5 years, which
  falls within the 43-month (3.…" With long first: "option a) offers a significantly higher return within the 1.59-year
  constraint" (a) = 2 years). The reasoning follows the answer in this format, so it is post hoc.

Reading: in Qwen3-14B the unit-match effect is real where it was measured, but it is one corner of a broader failure to
apply the horizon constraint: the constraint is honoured mainly when the short option is listed first and the delays
are in months or years. Reward ratio and D interact irregularly with unit (e.g. short first, H/D < 0.85: "261 weeks"
0.66 at ratio 5 vs 0.13 at ratio 20; "20 quarters" 0.26 vs 0.71), not interpreted.
