## Key decisions

- **First dataset = replication of the preprint's single-turn intertemporal choice**, not the
  proposal's multi-turn planning protocol. Reason: the deck framed weeks 1–4 as exploration and
  Ian suggested prototyping on Qwen3-14B; a replication yields data for both chosen directions
  (structure, behavior↔geometry) and a direct comparison with the 4B results. Rejected: building
  the multi-turn pipeline first (presumes an unchosen direction).
- **Capture = batched greedy generation, then an unpadded per-sample teacher-forced pass with
  forward hooks on the embedding and every decoder layer.** Rejected: `output_hidden_states`
  (its last entry is post-norm; relative L2 vs raw last-layer residual 0.90) and padded batched
  forward for capture (bf16 kernel noise: worst-position relative L2 error 0.6% at layer 1 rising
  to 8% at layer 35 vs the single-sequence reference; median 0.4%→1.4%). Unpadded per-sample
  passes reproduce the library's hidden states bit-exactly (max abs diff 0.0, all 36 layers, 4
  samples, Qwen3-8B). Batched generation kept for speed; identical greedy tokens under batch 1 vs 4
  on the 4 checked samples, but near-ties can differ, so the generated text is the unit of record.
- **Label logits in float32** from the normed residual (two rows of `lm_head`), `p_short` =
  sigmoid(logit_short − logit_long). Reason: bf16 logits at |logit|≈60 have ulp 0.5, and one
  near-tied sample moved from p=0.32 to 0.44 between batched and unbatched passes.
- **Explicit 2-GPU device map for Qwen3-14B** (`--split 19`: embeddings + layers 0–18 on GPU 0,
  layers 19–39 + norm + lm_head on GPU 1). accelerate `device_map="auto"` put 15.11 GiB on GPU 1
  and OOMed; with max_memory caps 13.4+14.3 or 13.9+14.7 GiB it silently offloaded `lm_head` to
  disk (params on `meta`) — `load_model` now raises if any parameter is off-GPU.
- **Stored layer axis**: 0 = embeddings, l = residual after decoder layer l−1, no final norm; all
  41 layers stored (bf16), ~7 MB/sample.
- **Sweep is layer-outer** (one sequential shard read per layer): full 697-cell sweep in 2m54s.
