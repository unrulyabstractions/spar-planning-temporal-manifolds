## Option-delay-unit control: is the years effect a match with the options' unit? (b125031)

Continues progress-20261002.md (years renderings lower-dimensional than months at matched durations). Alan raised the
confound I flagged in the session review: every prompt states the option delays in years ("1 year", "10 years"), so a
horizon in years is in the options' unit while other units need conversion. Alan's framing: if units change the character
of the local scatter around the manifold, that may be a thread for this project or a follow-on.

Design (2×2, matched durations = tier-A month base 1..1200, n = 1200 per cell, same scenario and layout otherwise):
option delays {years: "1 year"/"10 years", months: "12 months"/"120 months"} × horizon rendering {integer months, years with
two decimals}. The years-delay cells already existed (tier-A months rows; runs/qwen3-14b_matched_months_years_dec2_n1200).
New prompts differ from the corresponding years-delay prompts only in the two option lines (lines 2 and 3; checked for the
months set), durations identical (horizon_years equal to tier A).
```
.venv/bin/python scripts/gen_dense_horizons.py data/prompts/matched_months_months_dm_n1200.parquet --matched-base months --n 1200 --max 1200 \
  --units months --render plain --short-delay "12 months" --long-delay "120 months"
.venv/bin/python scripts/gen_dense_horizons.py data/prompts/matched_months_years_dec2_dm_n1200.parquet --matched-base months --n 1200 --max 1200 \
```

Line R², month delays: months 0.313–0.521, years 0.353–0.608.

Reading:
- The dimension effect is not the options' unit: with delays in months, years renderings are still lower-dimensional than
  months at every cell, by 4.8–9.3 (year delays: 4.2–6.7). Matching the option unit changes the choice, not this.
- Geometry and behaviour dissociate: the match between horizon and option units drives the choice (0.99 vs 0.73–0.78), while
  the years/months dimension gap is present whether or not the units match.
- The gap is in the local scatter, not the global spread: with month delays the Gaussian-matched values of years and months
  differ by only 0.2–5.7 while the estimates differ by 4.8–9.3, and the scatter left after the smooth curve is lower for
  years by 4.1–11.5 in both conditions. Years renderings have a comparable covariance spectrum but a lower-dimensional
  neighbourhood structure.
- The small curve-likeness advantage of years (+0.035 to +0.077 at three cells with year delays) shrinks to +0.002 to +0.030
  with month delays: not a robust property.
- Delays in months raise every estimate (H months +1.6 to +6.9, H years +1.3 to +3.0), i.e. the context changes the scatter
  of both renderings; the years-months ordering is unchanged.
- Caveat: years is rendered with two decimals here (needed for matched durations); integer years were also lower than months
  in tier B (n = 100) and in the digit-count control (same digit strings), so the ordering holds across years formats.

Thread for this project or a follow-on (Alan's framing): what about a years rendering makes the local scatter
lower-dimensional — the unit token, its frequency in long-horizon contexts, the way the model converts (reasoning restates
years directly but converts months), or the decimal format — and whether the scatter's character (not only its dimension)
differs, e.g. its spectrum, its alignment with the curve's normal space, or how it varies along the curve.

Not done: days/weeks under month delays; integer years at n = 100 under month delays; 27B; second scenario.
