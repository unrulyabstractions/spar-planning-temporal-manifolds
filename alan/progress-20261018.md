## Thinking conclusion vs final answer, full pilot (all three models)

Question (Alan): how prevalent are mismatches between the thinking and the final answer? Before today only 7 traces
(Qwen3-14B violations) had been compared; 2 contradicted.

Tooling on this laptop: no Python; installed uv (user profile, ~/.local/bin) and ran with
`uv run --no-project --with pandas --with pyarrow`. Data: the fetched merged think-pilot index.parquet files
(runs/vast_fetch_20261017{,b,c}/runs/*_think_pilot/), 3 × 384 rows; closed thinking 379 / 362 / 384 = 1,125.

Method: scripts/think_conclusion_audit.py (this commit). Rule A = last strong commitment inside the thinking ("I choose:",
"the answer is/must be", "the best/safest choice is", "option X is better/preferable", "so X." …) with hypotheticals
dropped ("maybe", "if", "could" … in the preceding 30 characters); rule B = last option label in the final 400 characters.
A first version (one rule, no hypothetical filter) flagged 15 rows; reading them all found 3 real contradictions and 12
parser errors (hypotheticals matched, "must be"/"safest choice" missed), which led to v2.

Hand audit (thinking tail and answer start read for every row listed):
- All 46 rows where rule A ≠ the answer or found nothing (14B 14, Qwen3.8 30, Gemma 2): 3 real contradictions, all 14B.
  The rest: conclusions phrased as "the second option is better" (14B), the format template "I choose: <a) or b)>"
  matched (Gemma 2, Qwen3.8 most), Qwen3.8 drafts "Could say I choose: b) …" dropped as hypothetical, "could choose a with
  caveat". All agree with the answer on reading.
- Random 10 per model (seed 0) from rows where rule A agrees and rule B does not (170 rows): 30/30 agree.
- Random 10 per model (seed 0) from rows where both agree (909 rows): 30/30 agree.

**Result: 3 contradictions in 1,125 closed traces, all Qwen3-14B (3/379); Qwen3.8-27B 0/362, Gemma 4 31B 0/384.**
- ub_D10_x5_lf_p0_Hmonths_dmonths_11: thinking "Therefore, the answer is b). But I'm not sure." → answers a).
- ub_D10_x5_sf_p0_Hyears_dquarters_11: thinking "Therefore, the answer is a). I think that's the safest conclusion." → b).
- ub_D10_x5_lf_p0_Hquarters_dmonths_11 (new): thinking "Therefore, the answer is option b)." → a).
All three are at H/D 0.983 (118 vs 120 months); in each the thinking concludes the short (correct) option and the answer
picks the long one, so 3 of 14B's 12 thinking violations are answer–thinking contradictions. Two of the three thinking
traces end hedged ("But I'm not sure", "the safest conclusion"). Gemma copies its in-thinking "I choose:" draft into the
answer; Qwen3.8 drafts the answer sentence in the thinking and outputs it.
Bounds: unread rows are ones where the parser agrees; with 0 hidden contradictions in 30 sampled per stratum, a rough
95% upper bound is ≈ 10% of each stratum (rule of three), i.e. a loose bound, not a precise rate. Truncated traces (14B 5,
Qwen3.8 22) have no answer and are excluded.
