allows both options").

**Reading:** for Qwen3-14B the mismatched-unit failure is a non-thinking-mode failure. With thinking, the model converts

horizon" counts, plus a few answer/reasoning contradictions. Scope: one D (10 y), one ratio (5), one phrasing, greedy,
cells of 8–24 prompts per bin.
